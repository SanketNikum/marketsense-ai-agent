What Moves a Stock Price Day to Day

In the short term, stock prices move based on the balance between buyers and
sellers, which is influenced by several common triggers:

1. Earnings results - a company beating or missing analyst expectations for
   revenue or profit often causes a sharp same-day move.
2. Sector-wide news - a regulatory change, interest rate move by the RBI, or
   global commodity price shift can move an entire sector at once.
3. Broad market sentiment - if the Nifty or Sensex is falling broadly due to
   global cues (like US Federal Reserve decisions or crude oil prices), most
   stocks tend to fall together regardless of their individual fundamentals.
4. Company-specific news - management changes, product launches, litigation,
   or a major client contract can move a single stock independent of the
   broader market.
5. Unusually high trading volume alongside a price move is generally a signal
   that the move reflects real, widespread investor conviction rather than a
   few small trades.
