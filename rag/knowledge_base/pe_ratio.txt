Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio

The P/E ratio compares a company's stock price to its earnings per share (EPS).
It is calculated as: Stock Price / Earnings Per Share.

A high P/E ratio suggests investors expect strong future growth and are willing
to pay more today for each rupee of current earnings. A low P/E ratio can mean
the stock is undervalued, or that investors expect earnings to decline.

P/E ratios are most useful when compared within the same industry, since
different sectors naturally trade at different average P/E levels. For example,
IT services companies often trade at different P/E multiples than banks.
